# Gifty Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Gifty ("the Bot") is a Discord giveaway bot. This policy explains what data the Bot processes and why.

## Data we process

- **Discord user IDs and usernames** of members who click the join button on a giveaway. This is stored so the giveaway can be run and a winner can be announced.
- **Giveaway information**: message ID, prize name and the role requirements of each giveaway.
- **Message content (temporary)**: the Bot reads the text of messages sent in servers where it is present, only to check whether a message contains certain keywords (for example "giveaway"). If it does, the Bot replies with a link to the owner's contact page. Message content is processed in memory only. It is **not stored, logged, copied or shared** with anyone.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information or any data outside Discord.

## How we use data

Data is used only to run giveaways (join/leave, participant list, winner announcement) and to provide the keyword reply feature. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

## Storage and retention

Giveaway participant lists are kept on the Bot owner's private server. A participant list is deleted when the giveaway is ended by an administrator. Participants can also remove themselves at any time by clicking the join button again.

## Your rights

You can ask what data is stored about you or request its deletion at any time by contacting the owner (see below).
